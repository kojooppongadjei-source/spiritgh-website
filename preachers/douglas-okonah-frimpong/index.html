About

Douglas Okonah Frimpong is the General Overseer of Centre for Christian Outreach Ministries. He also serves as Executive Board Chairman of Faith Convention and Planning Committee Chairman of the Kumasi Council of Christian Churches.

In 55 years of active ministry, he served for many years as Ghana Director of Morris Cerullo World Evangelism, and most recently as Donor Relations and Resource Mobilization Manager of the Ghana Institute of Linguistics, Literacy and Bible Translation (GILLBT).

His passion is to raise Christian leaders and mobilise the Church for revival, evangelism and missions. He has helped more than 10,000 young men and women in Ghana and beyond into ministry.
