About the Bishop

Bishop Noble Francis Afotey Odai is the Founder and General Overseer of Maranatha Power Ministries International, which he has led since 1996. Under his leadership the ministry has grown to more than 100 branches with nearly 300 pastors across Ghana.

He is known for his passion for leadership development and for humanitarian projects that serve the most vulnerable in society: street children, the aged, widows and widowers, and people living with disabilities.

Trained as a mechanical engineering technician at Accra Polytechnic, he went on to found schools, a Bible college, a television station and several enterprises, and now chairs the board of the Postal and Courier Services Regulatory Commission.

He is married to Rev. Valarie Afotey Odai, and they are blessed with three children: Rev. Francis Odai Afotey Jnr, Ruth Afotey Odai and Deborah Korklu Afotey Odai.

Leading the ministry

As General Overseer, Bishop Afotey Odai leads the church and its branches, sets policy and strategy, guards sound doctrine, and oversees the ministry's finances, administration and people. He has organised leadership conferences in Ghana, the United States, Switzerland and India.
