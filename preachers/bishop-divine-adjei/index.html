About the Bishop

Bishop Dr. Divine Adjei is the founder of Calvary Life Chapel International, a ministry with congregations around the world. He also serves as Presiding Bishop of the Wesley Synod, Northern Sector of Ghana.

An apostle by calling, he is known for a strong teaching ministry. He is a church planter who has established many churches over more than thirty years in ministry, and he speaks at conferences and leads seminars to train leaders.

He is married to Apostle Mrs. Ida Adjei, and God has blessed them with four daughters.

Current roles

Ministry

His ministry centres on teaching the Word, planting churches and developing leaders who can carry the work forward.
